Eight ways to make it feel alive

04

Circuit

Each subject is a parallel circuit. Finishing a topic closes its switch: current flows through that branch and its bulb lights up. (Parallel, so every bulb works on its own, just like in P2.)

Where it goes: a subject's overview. Works with the periodic table: the table shows the whole course, the circuit zooms into one subject.

Light up Biology

Tap a topic to close or open its switch.

 

05

Constellation

Your course is a night sky. Every topic is a star. When you finish topics in a subject, their stars brighten and join up, so each subject draws its own constellation over the year.

Where it goes: a "My progress" page. It's an alternative to the periodic table as the main map, so you'd pick one of the two.

Your sky so far

Example progress. Tap a star to finish or un-finish that topic.

 

06

DNA strand

Your blurting results as a slowly turning double helix. Each rung is one question, coloured green, amber or red. A strong blurt is a strand that's nearly all green.

Where it goes: the blurting results screen, instead of the row of coloured squares. Works with the periodic table: the tile shows your score, the strand shows the detail.

07

Titration

The moment your answers are marked, your score drips from a burette into a conical flask. Like phenolphthalein, the flask stays colourless until the end point: 80% or more turns it pink.

Where it goes: the moment a blurt is marked. Works with the periodic table: the score that fills the flask is the score that fills the tile.

08

Oscilloscope

Your revision habit as a heartbeat on a green oscilloscope screen. Each day you revise adds a beat, and longer sessions give bigger beats. A day off is a flat line.

Where it goes: the top of the homepage, beside "pick up where you left off". Works with everything: it tracks the habit, not the topics.
