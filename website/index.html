How a star gets brighter

How it all works →
  1. Read the notes

    Short notes with diagrams and worked examples. Read them all and your star is Shining.

  2. Blurt it

    Close the notes and answer 20, 25 or 30 fresh questions from memory. Now it's Bright.

  3. Get it marked

    Green, amber or red for every answer, with what you missed. 80% or more and it's Blazing.

  4. Join the dots

    Light up the stars in a subject and they join into its constellation.
