How it works

  1. Read the notes

    Short notes on one part of the spec, with diagrams, worked examples and key words.

  2. Blurt it

    Close the notes and answer 20, 25 or 30 fresh questions from memory.

  3. Get it marked

    Each answer comes back green, amber or red, with what you missed and a model answer.

  4. Watch your sky

    Every note and blurt makes a star brighter, until each subject is a full constellation.
