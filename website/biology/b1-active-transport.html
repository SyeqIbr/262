Active transport

Active transport moves substances from a more dilute solution to a more concentrated solution: against the concentration gradient. This needs energy from respiration.

  • Root hair cells take in mineral ions from very dilute soil water, for healthy growth.
  • The gut absorbs sugar into the blood even when there is more sugar in the blood than in the gut. The sugar is then used for respiration.
