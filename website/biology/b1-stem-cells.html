Stem cells

A stem cell is an undifferentiated cell that can divide to make more cells like itself, and can differentiate into other types of cell.

TypeFound inCan become
Embryonic stem cellsHuman embryosAlmost any type of cell
Adult stem cellse.g. bone marrowMany types of cell, including blood cells
MeristemTips of plant roots and shootsAny type of plant cell, throughout the plant's life

Uses

  • Stem cells may help treat conditions such as diabetes and paralysis.
  • In therapeutic cloning, an embryo is made with the same genes as the patient. Its stem cells are not rejected by the patient's body.
  • Meristem tissue can clone plants quickly and cheaply, e.g. to save rare species from extinction or to grow crops with useful features such as disease resistance.

Issues

  • Risk of transferring viral infections with the stem cells.
  • Some people have ethical or religious objections to using embryos.
