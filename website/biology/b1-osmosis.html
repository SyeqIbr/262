Osmosis

Osmosis is the diffusion of water from a dilute solution to a concentrated solution through a partially permeable membrane.

  • A dilute solution has lots of water and little dissolved substance.
  • A concentrated solution has less water and more dissolved substance.

Exam tip: Always say "water" and "partially permeable membrane". Leaving either out can cost a mark.

Required practical: osmosis

  • Cut equal-sized potato cylinders. Blot them dry and weigh each one.
  • Put each one in a different concentration of salt or sugar solution.
  • After a set time, blot them dry and weigh them again.

% change in mass = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100

Mass goes up in dilute solutions (water moves in) and down in concentrated ones (water moves out).

Plot % change in mass against concentration. Where the line crosses zero, the solution has the same concentration as the potato cells, so there is no overall movement of water.
