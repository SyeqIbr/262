Osmosis & active transport

In this note you'll learn to

  • Define osmosis and predict which way water will move
  • Describe what happens to plant and animal cells in different solutions
  • Describe the osmosis required practical
  • Explain active transport and compare it with diffusion and osmosis

Osmosis

Osmosis is the diffusion of water from a dilute solution to a concentrated solution through a partially permeable membrane. The membrane lets water through but not larger dissolved particles.

  • A dilute solution has a lot of water and a little dissolved substance.
  • A concentrated solution has less water and more dissolved substance.

Common mistake: saying "particles" move in osmosis. It's always water, and always through a partially permeable membrane. Leaving either out can cost a mark.

Required practical: osmosis

Aim: investigate how the concentration of a salt or sugar solution affects the mass of plant tissue.

  1. PrepareCut equal-sized potato cylinders. Blot them dry and weigh each one.
  2. SoakPut each cylinder in a different concentration of solution, e.g. 0.0 to 1.0 mol/dm³.
  3. WaitLeave them for the same time (e.g. 24 hours) at the same temperature.
  4. Re-weighBlot them dry, weigh them again and calculate the percentage change in mass.

% change in mass = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100

Using % change means you can compare cylinders that started at different masses.

Plot % change in mass against concentration. Mass goes up in dilute solutions and down in concentrated ones. Where the line crosses zero, the solution has the same concentration as the potato cells.

Active transport

Active transport moves substances from a more dilute solution to a more concentrated solution: against the concentration gradient. This needs energy from respiration.

  • Root hair cells use it to take in mineral ions from very dilute soil water. Plants need these ions for healthy growth.
  • The gut uses it to absorb sugar into the blood, even when there's more sugar in the blood than in the gut. Cells then use the sugar for respiration.

Key words

Osmosis
The diffusion of water from a dilute to a concentrated solution through a partially permeable membrane.
Partially permeable
Lets some substances (like water) through, but not others.
Turgid
Describes a plant cell that is swollen and firm with water.
