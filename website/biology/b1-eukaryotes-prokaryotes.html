Eukaryotes & prokaryotes

Plant and animal cells are eukaryotic: their genetic material is enclosed in a nucleus. Bacterial cells are prokaryotic: they have no nucleus, and they are much smaller than eukaryotic cells.

Orders of magnitude

Scientists compare sizes using orders of magnitude. Each order of magnitude is a factor of 10. If one cell is 100 times wider than another, it is 2 orders of magnitude bigger (because 10 × 10 = 100).
