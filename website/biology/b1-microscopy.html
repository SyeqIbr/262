Microscopy

Light vs electron microscopes

Light microscopes let us see cells and their nucleus. Electron microscopes have a much higher magnification and a much higher resolution: the ability to tell two points that are close together apart. This means they can show small structures, such as ribosomes and the inside of mitochondria, in detail. They have helped scientists understand what cells are made of.

ResolutionLowerMuch higher

Calculating magnification

magnification = image size ÷ real size

Rearranged: real size = image size ÷ magnification. Both sizes must be in the same unit.

Converting units

mm × 1000 → µm × 1000 → nm

To go the other way (nm → µm → mm), divide by 1000 at each step.

Worked example 1

An image of a cell is 5 mm wide. The real cell is 0.05 mm wide. What is the magnification?

5 ÷ 0.05 = 100

Magnification = × 100

Worked example 2

A cell's image is 20 mm wide at a magnification of × 400. What is the real width in µm?

Real size = 20 ÷ 400 = 0.05 mm

0.05 mm × 1000 = 50 µm

Real width = 50 µm

Common mistake: dividing numbers that are in different units. Convert first, then divide. Magnification has no units, but always write it with a × sign.

Required practical: microscopy

Aim: use a light microscope to observe, draw and label plant or animal cells.

  1. Prepare the slidePlace a thin layer of tissue (e.g. onion epidermis) on a slide. Add a drop of stain such as iodine, so the parts show up.
  2. Add a coverslipLower it on at an angle to avoid trapping air bubbles.
  3. FocusStart with the lowest-power objective lens. Focus with the coarse knob, then the fine knob. Then switch to a higher power.
  4. RecordDraw what you see with a sharp pencil: clear lines, no shading. Label the parts and add the magnification or a scale bar.

Key words

Magnification
How many times bigger the image is than the real object.
Resolution
The ability to tell apart two points that are close together.
Micrometre (µm)
One thousandth of a millimetre.
Stain
A dye added to a specimen so its parts are easier to see.
